Shipping & Delivery

Q.What shipping options does QyHome offers?

We offer several shipping options such as courier,home delivery and store pick-up service.

Q.When will I receive my order?

Home Delivery for Peninsular Malaysia will take from 3 to 7 working days, unless stipulated otherwise. Please note that home delivery for East Malaysia delivery will take longer time up to 21 working days. Store pick-up only available to the the area at the Penisular Malaysia within 3 to 7 days, You'll be notified via email when your order is ready for pickup.

Q.How do I track my order status?

You can track your order by clicking into the 'Tracking Order' button at the top of the page. Your order delivery status will be display on the page.

Order & Payment

Q.What payment method that our store receive ?

We receive credit card/debit card , eWallet such as Touch N Go, Boost, GrabPay,Shopee Pay and also online bank transfer.

Q.Will I receive any order confimation after placing an order?

Yes, a confirmation email will be sent to the buyer email upon completed payment.

Q.Will the QyHome acceptable COD?

No, cash on delivery is currently not available. Please choose other available payment method.

Q.How do I place an order?

To place an order, simply select the product(s) you'd like to purchase and add them to cart. Then fill in your delivery details if you've never shopped with us before, and proceed to checkout.

Q.How can I know if my payment is successful or failed?

For a successful payment, a confirmation email from ipay88 will be send to your email.

Q.Which credit card/debit card are accepted from payment?

All credit/debit cards issued from major banks in Malaysia are accepted.

Cancellations,returns,refunds and exchange

Q.How can I cancel my order?

To cancel your order, you'll need to sign in and click into your "Order History" , then search for the order you'd like to cancel.

Q.How can I return an item?

To return large home appliances, you may contact our customer careline by call or email and we will arrange our delivery or courier personnel to collect the item from you. From small appliances, you may also return any of our outlet nearest to you.

Q.For which reason can I return an item?

Incorrect:The item is not the item you ordered. The model or color is different from what is indicated on the order summary, or there are missing item or parts inside the packaging.
Damaged: The item is found to be damaged upon receipt.
Defective: The product is not functioning as described by the manufacturer.
